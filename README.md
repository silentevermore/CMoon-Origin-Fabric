# C-Moon Origin Addon
Ever dreamt of playing as your favorite character from JJBA, C-Moon? Not a problem!

This addon brings the ability of turning your gameplay into something more entertaining, with the abilities of [GravityChangerAPI](https://github.com/qouteall/GravityChanger/) and [Origins](https://github.com/apace100/origins-fabric).
# Abilities
- **Gravity Switch:** you can change gravity in any direction you want, when secondary keybind is pressed
- **Inversional Punches**: when you hit a target entity, you cause a harmful effect
- **Protection Shield**: you can create a spherical gravity shield around you, protecting you from any kind of damage, for a short period of time
- **Lack of Lungs**: you cannot be drowned in any kind of fluid
- **Fall Damage Immunity**: you are completely invulnerable to fall damage
- **Extra Reach**: your reach distance is increased by default
- **Natural Armor**: you have additional armor
